## NIST comparison-input example

The file `data/sample/identitymf_minmax_sample.tsv` is derived from the NIST data associated with:

**Moorthy, Arun S., and Sisco, Edward (2021). Supplemental Data and Source Code for Min-Max Test Research. National Institute of Standards and Technology.** [Official record](https://data.nist.gov/od/id/mds2-2418).

Associated article: Moorthy, A. S., & Sisco, E. (2021). *The min-max test: an objective method for discriminating mass spectra.* Analytical Chemistry, 93(39), 13319–13325. [https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.analchem.1c03053](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.analchem.1c03053).

The distributed sample is a byte-for-byte copy of the user-supplied archive member `IdentityMF/IdentityMF_MinMax_fullResults.txt`. On 2026-11-23 it was renamed to `identitymf_minmax_sample.tsv` and placed in `data/sample/` to identify its incomplete scope; its numerical contents were not modified. Selection and provenance details appear in [data/README.md](data/README.md) and [docs/source_provenance.json](docs/source_provenance.json).
